From the hills of Himachal to the booths of Hyderabad.

Saurabh Kumar Dhiman left Himachal Pradesh for Hyderabad to chase one thing: the booth. He trained at Panache The DJ School, took his first residency at 10 Lounge in 2013, and has since played more than 50 venues across the city, along with rooms in Bangalore, Delhi and Goa.

He is an open-format DJ in the truest sense. A night can start on deep house and nu-disco, climb through tech house, and land on Bollywood, Punjabi and hip-hop when the floor asks for it. The skill is reading the room and moving it without a seam. He is currently resident at Tabula Rasa Alfresco Bar.
